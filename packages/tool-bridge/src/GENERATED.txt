parity.json and schedule.json ARE NOT EDITED BY HAND.
They are generated by executing the platform packages:
  node tools/extract-parity.mjs   <platform installation root> > src/parity.json
  node tools/extract-schedule.mjs <platform installation root> > src/schedule.json
Run this after every platform upgrade: the descriptions and schemas in there
change silently, and a divergence will give no sign of itself.
